# zenkari — Support

**zenkari** is an iPhone and iPad Akari (Light Up) puzzle app: place lanterns, light every open cell, solve numbered-wall constraints, and explore variations with procedural puzzles, bundled packs, imports, a daily challenge, and optional Game Center leaderboards and achievements. Core solo play runs on your device; no account is required unless you use Game Center features.

## Requirements

- **iPhone or iPad** with **iOS 17** or later
- **Game Center** (Apple ID) is optional and only needed for leaderboards, achievements, and the daily challenge leaderboard flow

## Help inside the app

Open **How to play** from the home screen or settings for a step-by-step tutorial (lantern placement, light rays, numbered walls, and marks). Use **Settings** to tune appearance (system / light / dark), sound, haptics, conflict highlighting, and the in-game timer. **Browse puzzles** offers bundled and imported content; **Play** generates a new board from your chosen variation and size.

If you enable **Game Center**, sign in from the home screen or settings to access leaderboards, achievements, and today’s challenge.

## Reporting a problem or requesting a feature

1. Check that you are on the **latest iOS** and the **latest app version** from the App Store.
2. Note your **device model**, **iOS version**, **puzzle variation**, and **board size** if the issue happens on a specific puzzle.
3. If something still looks wrong, use the **Support** contact method listed on the app’s App Store product page (Apple passes along the URL you configure in App Store Connect).

## Privacy

Solo play does not require a login. Saved puzzles, settings, and local progress stay on your device. **Game Center** features are provided by Apple when you choose to sign in. For advertising, analytics, and tracking disclosures, see the **[Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md)**. The App Store **App Privacy** section and in-app prompts (such as **App Tracking Transparency**) describe how data is used on your device.
