# zenkari

**A native Akari (Light Up) puzzle app for iPhone and iPad** — place lanterns, light every open cell, and solve logic grids with touch-friendly play, clear feedback, and a steady flow of puzzles.

## Why it exists

Akari is a classic Nikoli logic puzzle: numbered walls, orthogonal light rays, and no two lanterns shining at each other. **zenkari** brings that game to iOS with readable grids, haptics, accessibility support, and enough variety to keep daily play interesting.

## Highlights

- **Classic Akari rules** — place lanterns on open cells, satisfy numbered walls, and light the whole board without mutual line-of-sight conflicts.
- **Variations** — standard play plus modes such as coloured light, mirrors, twins, queens (diagonal rays), regional boards, and hex grids (availability may vary by release).
- **Fresh puzzles** — procedurally generated boards by difficulty and size, bundled puzzle packs, and import support.
- **Daily challenge** — a new puzzle each day with a target time (requires **Game Center** sign-in for leaderboards and challenge tracking).
- **Game Center** — leaderboards, achievements, and fastest-time previews per board size and variation (optional; core solo play does not require an account).
- **Tutorial and helpers** — step-by-step how-to-play, optional conflict highlighting, timer, marks, sound, and haptics.
- **Accessibility** — VoiceOver labels on the grid, Dynamic Type in navigation and settings, Reduce Motion support, and ads kept out of the primary assistive focus order.
- **No sign-in for solo play** — saved games, settings, and local progress stay on your device.
